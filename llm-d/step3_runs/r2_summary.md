## 5. Multi-Shape Disaggregation Physics & P:D Balance Takeaways

1. **`S2 = 1024 : 1024` (`1:1` Ratio — Decode-Bound):** Because `1,024`-token uncached prefill requires half the Pool A prefill compute per request vs `S1`, Pool A easily feeds Pool B faster than `1.0s` TTFT through `c=768`, allowing Pool B Decode (`8` chips) to pin its `max_num_seqs = 48` (`768` global slot) ceiling and reach **`44,166.04` out tok/s** (**`5,520.76` / decode chip**). On `1:1` uncached workloads, `1P:2D` or `1P:3D` (`R5`) will raise total-chip efficiency (`out tok/s / total chip`) by right-sizing Prefill capacity.
2. **`S1 = 2048 : 1024` (`2:1` Ratio — Near-Ideal `1P:1D` Uncached Match):** At `c=768..1024`, `8` Prefill chips (`96.3k` in tok/s) and `8` Decode chips (`46,822.96` out tok/s = `5,852.87` / decode chip, `143,079` total `in+out` tok/s) co-saturate almost simultaneously (`~12.0k` prompt tok/s/prefill-chip matched to `5.85k` out tok/s/decode-chip at `2:1`).
3. **`S3 = 8192 : 1024` (`8:1` Ratio — Strictly Pool A Prefill Compute Bound):** At `0.00%` prefix cache hit, every `1,024` output tokens require `8,192` uncached prefill tokens (`8x` prefill compute per output token vs `S2`). Pool A (`8` Prefill chips) saturates at **`214,794.24` prompt tok/s** (`26,849.3` prompt tok/s/prefill-chip), capping output throughput at **`26,229.45` out tok/s** (`3,278.68` / decode chip) while Pool B Decode sits lightly loaded (`22.38` seqs/DP rank) and consequently sustains ultra-fast per-user decode speeds (`75.50` tok/s/user median even at peak `S3` concurrency!). In production `8K+` agentic workloads, prefix caching (`R4`) removes `85–95%` of this redundant prefill compute and shifts the operating regime back toward `S2`/`R3` decode efficiency.
